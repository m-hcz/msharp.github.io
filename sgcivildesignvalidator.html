SG Civil Design Validator

 Windows

Web:

 sg.ccsupport.cz/sgcivildesignvalidator

Used technologies:

C#, .NET4.8, .NET8, WPF, WebView2

Description:

Add-on for Autodesk Civil 3D. Helps users to validate their design based on Czech standards.

SlopeGuard:

Validates the designed slopes of a corridor against the design speed and lists errors and warnings by station.

Input parameters:

Station ranges, design speed and corridor feature lines are set before validation.
