SG Civil Tools

 Windows

Web:

 sg.ccsupport.cz/sgciviltools

Used technologies:

C#, .NET4.8, .NET8, WPF, WebView2

Description:

Add-on for Autodesk Civil 3D. Helps users to design linear structures based on Czech standards.

Staking point generator:

Generates staking points along a corridor and exports them to COGO and XLS.

Alignment table:

Lists stations, coordinates and radii of an alignment and exports the table to XLS, TXT or DWG.
