SG Civil BIM Complete

 Windows

Web:

 sg.ccsupport.cz/sgcivilbimcomplete

Used technologies:

C#, .NET4.8, .NET8, WPF, WebView2

Description:

Add-on for Autodesk Civil 3D. Helps users to create and validate BIM data.

BIM Complete:

Fills the BIM properties of a project and its elements according to a data standard.

Property sets validation:

Checks property sets and reports missing and empty values.
