CC Cad Tools

 Windows

Web:

 cccadtools.cadconsulting.cz  Autodesk App Store

Used technologies:

C#, .NET4.8, .NET8, WPF

Description:

Add-on for all Autodesk AutoCAD-based products. Implements features that are not available in the software.

File Manager:

Pins drawings, folders and templates in one palette for quick opening.

Milling & Stripping:

Creates a stripped surface from a selected surface and boundary layers.
